## Verdict

The foundations are better than most professional portfolios: a measured token
system, contrast written down wherever a choice was made, a strict lime rule,
reduced-motion paths everywhere, and careful copy. **The surface does not yet
match that work.** A recruiter scrolls about 2.8 screens to reach the first
project. The strongest evidence (3,000+ investors, 614 tests, −85% memory) sits
at the bottom of the case studies. And a few unfinished things are visible: a
blank portrait box, a broken 404, copy that contradicts itself.

No new direction is needed. The Swiss ground with poster moments is right. The
job is to make the first five seconds do the recruiter's job, put proof ahead of
prose, and finish the details.

## Protect — do not change these

- The token system in `src/styles/global.css`, the lime rule, and the
  Lighthouse accessibility budget at exactly 1.00.
- The opening screen that writes the signature: once per visit, never blocks,
  cannot strand the page.
- The footer's full-bleed static wordmark on black. The best poster moment on
  the site.
- The tilted menu card, the hairline tables, and lime used once per page.
- Case-study and post prose at 17px / 1.6 in a measured column, and the honest,
  specific voice.
- The radius rule: controls fully round, panels 16px, structure square.

## Priority 0 — visible defects

Fix these before anyone else sees the site.

### 0.1 The About page shows a blank 633px grey box — done in PR #23

`src/pages/[...lang]/about.astro:115` still renders `Placeholder` where the
portrait goes. The photograph exists (the portrait JPEG in `raw-assets/`), but at
720×720 it cannot fill the full-bleed 16:9 slot the page reserves.
`raw-assets/` is ignored by git, so the file is only on my machine; it gets
copied into `src/assets/` as part of this fix.

**Decision.** Drop the full-bleed slot. Show the portrait:

- as a 4:5 crop, at most 360px CSS wide, since 720px of source covers that at 2×;
- in the left column under the About standfirst from `lg`, and above the bio on
  a phone;
- in monochrome (greyscale, contrast about 1.05), so the warm wood behind it does
  not compete with the lime;
- with square corners, because it is content, not a panel;
- from `src/assets/`, through Astro `<Image>`.

**Done when** `LAUNCH=true pnpm check:arch` reports no placeholders, and no width
shows an empty field.

### 0.2 The 404 is still the starter page — done in PR #23

`src/pages/404.astro` sets its title in `text-h-l font-emph`, puts a monospace
"404" above it, and renders two `Button`s that balloon into ovals about 120px
tall.

**Decision.** Rebuild it in the site's own language: a display-size line, one
line of `text-read`, and the two pill controls used everywhere else
(`src/components/ui/Pill.astro`). Left-aligned on the page margin, not centred.

### 0.3 Copy contradicts what is on screen — done in PR #23

- `work.featuredDescription` (`src/i18n/ui.ts:105`, and `:280` in Indonesian)
  says three projects, above four rows. `home.workDescription` (`:53`, `:228`)
  says four.
- The next-project row on every case study prints "01". Its counter is
  hard-coded at `src/pages/[...lang]/work/[slug].astro:271`. Pass the project's
  real position, or hide the counter on that row.
- The WhatsApp bot is titled with its repository name, `personal-wa-bot`, in
  both `src/content/projects/{en,id}/wa-bot.mdx`. Give it a display title (item
  2 under "Content I still owe"), and keep the repository name in the source
  link.
- `about.historyDescription` (`ui.ts:82`, `:257`), "Fifteen years of it", reads as
  fifteen years of engineering. Suggested: "From architecture school in 2011 to
  the last thing I shipped."

**Decision on how many projects to feature.** Set `featured: false` on Bunda
Halimah. The home page then shows three, which is what the spec and the home
page's own comment intend. The Work page shows the full table of four plus three
detailed rows, and the table earns its place. Update both descriptions to match.
(The brand guide picks Nabitu, the bot and Norven. I may still swap Norven
for Bunda Halimah.)

### 0.4 The hero's scroll cue is close to invisible — done in PR #23

The `data-scroll-cue` element (`src/pages/[...lang]/index.astro:141`) is 21px in
`text-ink-invert-muted/60`. That is a dark-field token at 60% on white, about
#CBCBCB, roughly **1.6:1**. The spec asked for `--t-micro` in `--ink-muted`.

**Decision.** `text-label text-ink-muted` (5.10:1), chevron kept. Remove it
outright once the hero carries calls to action (1.1); a hero that asks for
something does not need a cue.

### 0.5 The project-row hover was invisible on grey sections — done in PR #15

Hover is now the picture zooming inside its frame plus an underline on the title,
which reads on every field.

## Priority 1 — the gap to top tier

### 1.1 The hero has to do the recruiter's job in five seconds — done in PR #24

**Seen.** At desktop sizes the top 56% of the hero is empty. Under the cropped
name, the one line saying what I do swaps with a 9.5–14px monospace class
string every eight seconds or so; on a phone that string wraps to three lines
and crowds the cue. There is no availability, no location, no call to action,
and the CV is one menu or one footer away.

**Decisions.**

- **Retire the looping code snippet** in `src/components/HeroStandfirst.astro`.
  The descriptor, "Frontend engineer · end to end", stays put in its current
  style. The joke costs the hero its clarity for half of every cycle. If it is
  wanted, it may play once after the opening screen (type, then resolve) and
  never loop. Static is the default.
- **Add an availability line in the empty upper band,** on the hero's 20% axis,
  about a third of the way down. `text-label`, uppercase, `--ink`, led by an 8px
  lime-500 dot (lime as a fill, so it is legal). Copy I still have to
  confirm (item 1 under "Content I still owe"), without em-dashes per the brand
  guide: "Open to frontend and full-stack roles · Remote or Indonesia · UTC+7".
- **Add two pill calls to action under the descriptor,** on the same axis. A dark
  pill, "Download CV", with "PDF, 108 KB", reusing `Pill` and the `siteConfig.cv`
  fields. A light pill, "Email me". They arrive on the existing `hero-enter`
  stagger.
- **On a phone,** the availability line, the wordmark, the descriptor, then the
  pills stacked full width. Let the hero grow to fit them rather than holding
  its fixed minimum height (`src/pages/[...lang]/index.astro:97`). The 360×640 overlap rule recorded in the hero's comment
  still applies.

**Done when** a five-second look at 1440×900 and at 390×844 gives the name, the
role, that I am available, and where the CV is, with nothing overlapping at
360×640.

### 1.2 Project rows should lead with proof

**Seen.** Each row shows a title, then one uppercase line that runs role and
stack together and wraps to two lines, then a 14px summary. The outcome figures
already in the content (`outcome.figures`) never appear in a row. Nothing says
the row is clickable.

**Decisions** (`src/components/ProjectRow.astro`).

- **Show the first two outcome figures** under the summary: the value at
  `text-h-l`, weight 300, with the caption at `text-micro` muted. For example
  "3,000+ investors · 70 pages", "614 tests · −85% memory", "WCAG AA gate ·
  $0.02 a month". This is the biggest single lever for an employer.
- **Split role from stack.** Role on its own line at `text-ui` in `--ink`. Stack
  on a quiet `text-micro` muted line, five items at most.
- **Set the summary at the new body size** (1.4).
- **Add an explicit affordance:** a `text-label` "Read the case study" with an
  arrow that nudges 4px on hover. The whole row stays the one link.

### 1.3 Screenshots — the stage and the 2× captures are done in PR #15

Shipped in PR #15:

- Every screenshot is mounted on a stage one step off its field, with a hairline.
- No screenshot is shown wider than two thirds of its pixel width, and none
  wider than 1,120px however sharp it is (`src/lib/screenshot.ts`).
- The case-study lead is a plate on a full-bleed band.
- The Norven capture is cut where its hero ends.
- All four website captures (Nabitu, Zeed, Norven, the hospital) are retaken
  at 2×, 2880 to 3200 pixels across. The hospital comes from my local build,
  not the live site. `handover.md` §3 says how each one was taken.
- The WhatsApp bot stays at 1× (1033×700) because no sharper capture exists.
  Its plate stops at 689px.

Still open:

- **Art-directed crops for phones,** through `<Picture>` with an optional mobile
  source in the project schema. At 390px a desktop capture is a thumbnail; a
  tighter crop, or the mobile UI, reads better. A tighter crop can be cut from
  the 2× files as they are. A capture of the mobile UI follows the same recipe
  at a phone viewport; the hospital's needs my local build running.

### 1.4 Body copy is set at fine-print size

**Seen.** Outside prose, everything persuasive is 14px: project summaries,
timeline descriptions, section descriptions, the capability notes and the
contact copy. Next to 300-weight display type at 96–150px, it reads as small
print.

**Decision.** Add `--text-body: 16px` at line-height 1.5 to `global.css`, and use
it for all of the above. `text-ui` (14px) stays for table cells, navigation,
footer links and metadata, and `text-read` (17px / 1.6) stays for long prose.

### 1.5 Case studies should put results first

**Seen.** The Outcome block comes after about 2,500px of prose, with its figures
at about 32px. Four figures wrap three-and-one and leave an orphan. "Visit the
live site" appears only at the very bottom, as a half-width empty box.

**Decisions** (`src/pages/[...lang]/work/[slug].astro`).

- **Add a key-results strip directly under the meta table,** with every figure at
  `text-display-s` over a `text-label` caption, as the original spec asked. One
  row of as many columns as there are figures (2, 3 or 4); two columns on a
  phone. Keep the outcome sentence at the end.
- **Put the live and source links in the header,** as light pills beside the meta
  table. The links block at the bottom can go.
- **Set prose headings at weight 500, not 600.** `prose-headings:font-emph`
  (`src/components/ui/Prose.astro:18`) borrows the inline-emphasis weight and
  makes them the heaviest type on the site. 500 keeps them scannable inside the
  light system.

### 1.6 The fixed controls run over reading content

**Seen.** The language and menu pills sit on top of headings and prose on case
studies, posts and the timeline, and constantly on a phone.

**Decision.** Past the hero, the right-hand controls hide on scroll down and come
back on scroll up, reusing the `data-mark-hidden` mechanism the signature already
uses in `src/components/Nav.astro`. They always show on focus and at the top of
the page, and have no transition under reduced motion. The CV stays one click
away because the menu returns on the first upward scroll.

### 1.7 Home page pacing

**Seen.** The hero takes a screen. Then a three-line centred statement sits in a
526px section with 192px of padding above and below, followed by another 192px
before "What I do". The first project begins about 2.8 screens down.

**Decisions.**

- The statement section goes to `space="lg"` and the stack section to
  `space="md"`.
- The statement left-aligns on the hero's 20% axis instead of centring. It is
  the only centred block on the site, and the shared axis ties it to the name
  above it.
- The statement's line-height goes from 0.95 to 1.05. At three lines, 0.95 nearly
  touches ascenders to descenders.

**Done when** the first project's image is visible within two screens at
1440×900.

### 1.8 The stack band's hierarchy is upside down

**Seen.** The domain names are display-size capitals, while the technologies a
recruiter is actually scanning for are 13px muted capitals. The lists sit inline
after the first two rows and wrap below the last two, which looks accidental.

**Decision** (`src/components/StackBand.astro`). Keep the big domain words; they
are the poster moment. Always put the list under the word, on its left edge, at
`text-body` in `--ink` and sentence case. Add the depth note from
`src/content/capabilities.json` ("Where I live", "Growing, deliberately") as a
`text-label` tag in front of the list. The drift is unchanged.

### 1.9 Link previews

**Seen.** Every page shares one `og-default.png`: the monogram and "farul.ivan",
with no role and no face. In a job search, the LinkedIn, Slack or email preview
is often the first impression.

**Decision.** Take preview images out of "deliberately deferred" and make one
1200×630 card per page, in Lexend and the site palette:

- home: name, role and the availability line;
- case studies: project title, the lead figure and the staged screenshot;
- posts: title and date.

Generate them at build time only if that ships no client JavaScript.
Hand-made PNGs in `public/` are acceptable.

## Priority 2 — polish

- **Section-header descriptions** are right-aligned paragraphs of up to five
  lines (`src/components/SectionHeader.astro:64`, `md:text-right`), which leaves
  a ragged left edge. Left-align them across columns 7 to 12 at `text-body`.
- **Timeline staircase** (`src/components/TimelineRow.astro`). The indent moves
  the whole row, so with seven rows on About the detail column narrows and slides
  under the menu pill. Indent only the year, and keep the detail column on one
  edge (column 7). Add a quiet `text-label` divider, "Before software", ahead of
  the fire-protection and architecture rows, so the career change reads as a
  story rather than a list.
- **The footer's CV control** (`src/components/Footer.astro:200`) is the only
  square, outlined control on the site. Make it the light-on-dark pill. The
  square CTA boxes on About and Contact stay; that emptiness is deliberate.
- **The language switch** (`src/components/LanguagePicker.astro`) shows the
  current locale ("EN") but links to the other one. Show "EN · ID" in the same
  44px pill, with the current one in `--ink` and the other muted.
- **Posts.** A three-item contents column on a two-minute post contradicts
  `pages.md` ("at this length it is noise"). Show contents only at four or more
  h2s, or six or more minutes; otherwise one measured column. Show the published
  date first and "Updated" second. Set topics as `text-micro` uppercase tags, to
  match the labels elsewhere.
- **Contact.**
  - Add a logistics block after Direct: roles sought, location and time zone,
    remote or relocation, earliest start, reply time.
  - Add a "Copy email" pill beside the large address; `mailto:` often opens
    nothing on a recruiter's machine.
- **Page transitions.** Use cross-document view transitions in CSS only
  (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`), with a shared
  `view-transition-name` from a row's screenshot to its case-study plate. No
  client router is needed, so the no-runtime rule holds. Gate it on
  `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- **Selection colour.** `::selection` is unset. Use lime-300 with `--ink` text;
  lime as a field with ink on it is legal.
- **A domain address.** `farulivan@gmail.com` undersells a site on its own
  domain. Routing `hi@farulivan.com` costs nothing. I have not decided on this one yet.

## Order of work

One batch per pull request, never mixed, each branched from `main`.

1. **fix:** visible defects, 0.1 to 0.4.
2. **feat:** the hero, 1.1.
3. **feat:** the body-size token, and project rows that lead with figures, 1.2
   and 1.4.
4. **feat:** case studies, results first, 1.5.
5. **feat:** fixed controls, home pacing and the stack band, 1.6 to 1.8.
6. **feat:** the polish list, phone crops (1.3), link previews (1.9) and view
   transitions.

Every one runs `pnpm verify`, adds strings to `en` first and then `id`, and is
looked at in both locales at 375, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 with reduced motion
on. CI only runs the Lighthouse budget on `main`, so run
`pnpm dlx @lhci/cli@0.15.1 autorun` locally before asking for review. It audits
every page three times, so allow more than ten minutes. Its report lands in
`.lighthouseci/`, which `.gitignore` does not cover, so delete it before
committing.
