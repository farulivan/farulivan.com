## The problem

The funnel report said the checkout converted badly and the team assumed the
form was too long. The session recordings said something else: people were
finishing each step and then losing the next one to a slow page load, on a
connection that could not afford three round trips.

## What I built

One page, three sections, no navigation between them. The address and payment
fields render immediately from cached data and validate as you go, and the only
network call before the final submit is the one that prices shipping.

Every field kept its own name and autocomplete attribute, which sounds like a
detail and accounted for a good part of the improvement — a browser that can
fill a form correctly saves more time than any amount of client-side
cleverness.
