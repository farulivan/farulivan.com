## The problem

The office had a tracking system and the drivers had WhatsApp, and the two
never met. Dispatchers reconciled them by hand in a spreadsheet that had grown
into the real system of record — the one everybody trusted and nobody could
back up.

## What I built

The console shows one map and one list, and they are the same data. Assigning a
job is a single action from either.

Most of the work was in the parts nobody demos. Position updates arrive over a
socket and are batched into animation frames, so a hundred moving vehicles cost
one repaint rather than a hundred. The client keeps a small queue of actions
taken while offline and replays them on reconnect, with conflicts resolved by
the server rather than by whoever reconnected last.

The phone build is the same code as the desktop one. There is no separate
mobile app to fall behind.
